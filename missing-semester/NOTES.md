# Teaching Notes

- Mission: become more effective in current .NET/React and Django development work through practical tool fluency.
- Baseline: the learner uses Git, CI/CD deployment, and AI coding agents at a basic level; most other course topics are familiar only through limited exposure or have not yet been implemented in day-to-day work.
- Pace: short 15–20 minute lessons a few times per week.
- Practice: safe, disposable WSL sandbox projects; do not use work repositories, credentials, or confidential ITDIV Binus / Top25 material.
- Teaching style: start from first principles, teach one small mental model, then use a practical task, retrieval practice, and immediate feedback.
- Context: connect examples to .NET/React and Django without replacing their framework-specific documentation.
- Scope: follow the official 2026 nine-topic syllabus in order. Do not create a `practice/` directory unless later lessons need persistent runnable artifacts.
- Learner preference (2026-10-19): read all lessons of a topic first for the big picture, then do quizzes and sandbox tasks afterwards. Lessons put reading first and a "Practise later" block before the quizzes.
- Topic 2 status (2026-10-19): learner read the official lecture and lesson 0002; lessons 0003–0005 written. Signals, SSH/tmux and dotfiles were new to the learner. Practice and retrieval for 0002–0005 still pending; no learning record until it's done.
