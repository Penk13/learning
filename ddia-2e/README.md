## Learning loop

Read a chapter in the book, then its short lessons. Each lesson is one idea, at most 5 minutes of reading, and ends in 2–4 cards that come back in the spaced-repetition review queue (see `DESIGN.md` at the repo root). The cheat sheet collects each chapter's key ideas, design-review questions and glossary terms.
